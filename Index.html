Controls (Desktop & Mobile Touch)
  • Aim Bow: Move Mouse, drag on Touch Screen, or use WASD / Arrow Keys.
  • Draw & Shoot: Hold Left Mouse Button (or hold Finger on screen) to draw the bowstring. Release to fire!

PHASE 3 — THE IGNITION (AMRIT KUND): Once all 10 heads burn, Ravana's central chest core opens. Loose the Final Agni Arrow into the glowing navel/heart vortex!

PHASE 4 — RAVANA DAHAN: Witness the grand choreographed fireworks, collapsing effigy, and festival celebration.
